Steps to Use Pre-Close NSC Online (NOT ALLOWED ONLINE AND AT POST OFFICE)
We cannot close an NSC one day before the actual maturity date using online banking or at the post office.
I opened the NSC on Mon 16-Aug-2021.
Maturity date: Sun 16-Aug-2026
When I attempted to pre-close the NSC on Sat 15-Aug-2026 at 04:09 PM IST (1 day before actual maturity date.)
the following error was displayed:
Account can be closed only on or after maturity.
Initially, login at: https://ebanking.indiapost.gov.in
Click General Services at the left pane.
Click Service Requests at the left pane.
Under Option at the right pane, select New Requests and click OK.
Select the NSC account that you want to close.
Select your post office account.
Click Submit Online.
When attempting to pre-close the NSC one day before the maturity date, the following error is displayed.
Account can be closed only on or after maturity.
Conclusion:
Pre-closure of the NSC is not permitted online one day before the maturity date. The system requires the account to be closed only on or after the maturity date.
