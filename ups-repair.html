Recommended Enclosure

Use a UV-resistant polycarbonate or GRP/FRP outdoor electrical enclosure with IP65/IP66 protection, lockable door and suitable ventilation.

Important

Do not use an ordinary household plastic storage box for the UPS. The enclosure should be designed for electrical equipment and outdoor/weather exposure.

Do not make the enclosure completely airtight. The UPS requires adequate ventilation and heat dissipation.

The washing machine purchase planned for December 2026 is not included in this estimate.

The final wire size should be selected by the electrician according to the UPS VA rating, current, cable length and voltage-drop requirements.
