When I log in daily during October 2026, the following alert appears immediately after login:
copilotapp.exe applicaion has not been provisioned
FILENAME:
C:\PROGRA~2\MICROS~1\Copilot\APPLIC~1\COPILO~1.EXE
1. Open Windows Task Manager (Ctrl + Shift + Esc).
2. Click the Startup apps tab.
3. Locate the relevant Copilot startup entry.
4. Right-click the entry and select Disable.
Sign out of Windows and sign in again to check whether the alert has stopped appearing.
