Important: PPF is a 15-year account. The calculation below assumes 8000.00 is deposited every month for the first 25 months and an additional 500.00 is deposited every month for the full 15 years.
PPF interest is calculated using an estimated monthly-compounding model for comparison purposes. Actual PPF interest is calculated according to the PPF rules and applicable financial-year interest rates.
PPF calculation: 8000.00/month × 25 months + 500.00/month × 180 months.
The total contribution is therefore 290000.00 over 15 years.
RD calculation: One Post Office RD with 8000.00 deposited every month for 5 years is compared with 80 separate RD accounts, each receiving 100.00 every month for 5 years.
Because 100.00 × 80 = 8000.00, both RD options have the same total monthly contribution and, assuming the same interest rate and dates, approximately the same maturity value.
SBI calculation: The SBI figure assumes 8000.00 is invested every month at a constant 6.00% annual rate for 25 months using the senioFD rates may change, so this is an estimate.
DNC calculation: DNC is shown using 8000.00 per month for 25 months. The calculation assumes the full chit value is received at the end without considering any dividend, discount, commission, GST, or other charges.
Rates: Post Office PPF 7.10% and Post Office RD 6.70% are the rates currently listed by India Post. Rates can be revised by the Government from time to time.
