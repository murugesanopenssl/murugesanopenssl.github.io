There is no fixed deposit at post office.
We do not need to go to post office to open a term deposit.
Term deposit needs to be opened for 1/2/3/4/5 years only.
We cannot open term deposit for few days/months using online or at post office.
We can open term deposit using post office internet banking.
Open following URL at any browser(firefox/msedge/opera/brave/netscape/chrome):
https://ebanking.indiapost.gov.in
Enter your user name and verification code.
Click Login.
